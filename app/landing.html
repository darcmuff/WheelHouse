What it does

SmartScanPaste the fill text from your broker's confirmation and it fills in the trade for you. Manual entry is there too.
Robinhood CSV importBring your history in from Robinhood's CSV export. It skips trades you already have and doesn't guess at rolls.
The wheel and LEAPs togetherPuts, assignment, covered calls and called away, plus LEAPs and the calls you sell against them (PMCC), all side by side.
Live captured profitEach open option shows how much of the premium you've already captured, kept up to date through the trading day.
Realised P&L onlyYour P&L only counts money you've actually closed out. No paper gains dressed up as profit.
A monthly 2% targetSee at a glance how this month is going against a steady 2% target on the money you've got working.
Pre-market and after-hoursSee where your tickers are trading before the open and after the close.
Your data is yoursExport everything to a spreadsheet any time, or delete your account and all of it in one go.

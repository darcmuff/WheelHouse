About OpTrack Bot

OpTrack Bot is a Grok Bot assistant that logs your trades for you. It reads your broker's trade confirmation emails and enters each trade in OpTrack through the normal app screens, just as you would.

What it does
  • Enters option and share trades on your schedule: wheels, puts, covered calls, LEAPs and poor man's covered calls.
  • Copies prices, fees, dates and fill times straight from your receipts.
  • Skips duplicates, and flags anything unclear instead of guessing.
  • Sends a short note after each run, with an optional weekly summary.
  • Can backfill older trades from past emails or a broker CSV.
Make it yours

It's a template. Set it up for your broker, then teach it in plain English, like "my TSLA calls go on the LEAP card" or "don't warn me about assignment". It remembers and adapts. If it helps you log trades, you can teach it to do it.

Your privacy

OpTrack Bot runs from your own Grok Bot account. It signs in to OpTrack as you and enters trades, but OpTrack itself never gets access to your email, your broker account or your logins. The bot only reads trade confirmations and only enters what you'd type yourself. It never deletes or closes anything.

About Me

I'm Marc. I'm semi-retired, and most of my working life was spent with my hands as a welder, an embroiderer and then a self-employed handyman. These days OpTrack headquarters is a caravan somewhere in Cornwall.

In 2024 I had a stroke. I used to be good with my hands. Now I'm just good with my hand. It slowed me down, but it also gave me time to learn new things. I taught myself options trading, and I wanted a simple way to keep track of my trades. Everything I tried was too fiddly or too expensive. So I taught myself to build apps and made OpTrack.

It does one job. You log your option trades, see what's open, and see what you've actually made. There's no hype, no tips and no nonsense.

I use it every week myself. If you've got an idea or something's not working, DM me on X. I'm always happy to hear from people.

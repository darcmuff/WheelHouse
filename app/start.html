      Questions

      What do I get?

      A tracker for the wheel and LEAPs on your phone and computer. Log a trade, follow it through rolls and assignment, and see your realised profit, return on capital and win rate. The free plan covers the basics; Pro takes the limits off and adds the extras listed above.

      Is it for beginners?

      Yes, if you already know what selling a put or a covered call is. It doesn't teach you to trade. It just keeps your record straight, which helps whether you've done ten trades or a thousand.

      Do I need to link my broker?

      No. You type trades in, paste a confirmation and let SmartScan fill it in, or import a Robinhood CSV. Nothing connects to your broker, and your data stays yours. You can export it or delete your account at any time.

      Can I cancel anytime?

      Yes. Cancel Pro from Settings whenever you like and you keep it until the end of the period you paid for, then drop back to the free plan. Your trades stay put.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. OpTrack records the trades you enter and does the sums. It doesn't tell you what to buy or sell, and prices can be delayed. Your trades are your call.

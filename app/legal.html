Not financial advice

WheelHouse is a record-keeping tool. Nothing in it is financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

It shows you numbers worked out from the trades you enter. It does not tell you what to buy or sell, and nothing in it is a recommendation. Prices, option values and Greeks come from delayed third-party feeds and may be wrong, late or missing. Figures such as profit, return and break-even are worked out automatically and may contain mistakes.

Options trading is risky and you can lose more than you put in. Always check figures against your broker's statements. Your broker's records are the official ones. If you need advice, speak to a qualified, FCA-authorised adviser.

Privacy policy

Who we are

WheelHouse is made and run by Marc Duff, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. Marc is the "data controller" for your information under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact: see the app's Settings.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, or your Google account's name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • What you enter: your trades, positions, notes and settings, and anything you import from a broker file.
  • Tickers you look up: the stock symbols in your account are sent to our price service to fetch quotes. They aren't linked to your name.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers (for example IP address and time of request) for security and to keep the service running.

We don't ask for, and you shouldn't enter, your broker login or bank details.

Why we use it

Only to run the app for you: sign you in, store your data and show your numbers. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (contract), and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure.

Where it is stored

Your account and data are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which may process data outside the UK under Google's standard data-protection terms. The website is hosted by Netlify. Live prices come from market-data providers (such as Finnhub, Cboe and Yahoo). They only see the ticker symbols, never your account.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising. We don't look at your trades except when you ask for help, or when we must to fix a fault.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted straight away from the live database. Backups held by our providers expire on their normal schedules.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data at any time:

  • Download: Settings → Export my data (JSON and CSV).
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and every trade, position and setting stored with it.

You can also contact us to use any of your other rights. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your settings (such as light/dark mode) and to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
